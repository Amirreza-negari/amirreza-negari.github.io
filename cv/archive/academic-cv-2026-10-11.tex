\section{Research Interests}
{\raggedright
\textbf{Quantum information:} Quantum Shannon theory and nonadditivity in quantum resource theories, spanning channel capacities, Holevo information and entanglement measures; Gaussian quantum information theory and the structure of quantum states and channels.\par
\vspace{3pt}
\textbf{Condensed matter:} How measurement and decoherence reshape topological and symmetry-protected phases and generate mixed-state order; interacting fermions and Hubbard models studied through analytical theory, tensor networks and determinantal quantum Monte Carlo.\par
\vspace{3pt}
\textbf{Statistical physics:} Statistical-mechanical mappings of error correction to disordered spin and loop models; noisy quantum memory thresholds, equilibrium and nonequilibrium criticality.\par}
